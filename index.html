I’m pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at PES University, Bengaluru, where I’ve maintained a 9.75/10 CGPA and received the Prof. MRD Scholarship for the top 5% of my cohort.

I tend to move between questions rather than stay inside one label: how models behave when the world shifts, how much information a multimodal system really needs, how an idea becomes a usable tool, and how technical concepts can be made understandable to someone else. Teaching has been a natural extension of that curiosity.

Outside computing, I’ve been learning Carnatic music for more than a decade. It has given me a different relationship with practice, structure, interpretation, and patience — interests that quietly carry over into how I approach technical work.
